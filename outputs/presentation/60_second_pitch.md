# GridFlex Local — 60-Second Technical Pitch

**Timing:** ~60 Seconds (approx. 150–165 words delivered at a calm, authoritative pace)  
**Target Audience:** Technical Judges, Utility Innovation Panels, Hackathon Evaluators  

---

### Spoken Pitch Script:

*"High penetration of rooftop solar and EVs is pushing low-voltage distribution feeders past their physical limits. At solar noon, excess generation drives severe voltage rise and backfeeds power into distribution transformers that were never built for reverse current.*

*GridFlex Local is a neighbourhood-scale flexibility coordination layer that turns local grid forecasts into dynamic operating envelopes for smart inverters, community batteries, and EV chargers.*

*Our fundamental breakthrough is **locational flexibility**. Conventional systems assume that if a feeder has 50 kilowatts of battery capacity, any 50-kilowatt problem is solved. But on resistive low-voltage cables, **available flexibility is not electrically useful flexibility**.*

*In our pandapower AC power-flow validation, GridFlex eliminates midday overvoltage—bringing voltage down from **1.1316 to 1.0146 p.u.**—and slashes reverse power flow by **92.5%**, from 187 kilowatts down to 14.*

*Yet when evening demand drops voltage at the terminal bus, our model honestly shows that a battery sitting at the substation cannot resolve downstream cable drop.*

*GridFlex does not assume that every available kilowatt is useful everywhere—it coordinates the right kilowatt at the right electrical location, proven by physics."*

---

### Delivery Cues for the Speaker:
1. **[0:00 - 0:12] Problem:** Speak clearly, stressing *reverse power* and *voltage rise*.
2. **[0:12 - 0:24] Solution:** State *dynamic operating envelopes* confidently.
3. **[0:24 - 0:38] Differentiator:** Slow down and punch the core phrase: *"available flexibility is not electrically useful flexibility."*
4. **[0:38 - 0:50] Evidence:** Quote the numbers precisely: *1.1316 to 1.0146 p.u.* and *92.5% reduction*.
5. **[0:50 - 1:00] Honesty & Closing:** Emphasize engineering realism: *"GridFlex does not assume that every available kilowatt is useful everywhere—it coordinates the right kilowatt at the right electrical location, proven by physics."*
